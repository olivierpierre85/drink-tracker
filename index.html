    How it counts

    1 standard drink (standaardglas / verre standard) = 10 g of pure alcohol.
    Grams = cl × % × 0,08 (alcohol weighs 0,8 g/ml), so 33 cl at 5% = 13,2 g = 1,3 standard drinks.

    Blood alcohol (g/L, the Belgian ‰) uses the Widmark formula: grams ÷ (weight × 0,68 for men or 0,55 for women). Each drink peaks about 30–40 minutes after you drink it. The body removes about 0,15 g/L per hour, and less at low levels. Levels: 0,5 don't drive (Belgian limit) · 0,8 drunk · 1,2 very drunk · 1,6 dangerous, with risk of blackout.
    It is only an estimate. Food, health, medication and tiredness change it a lot. Never use it to decide whether you can drive.

    ⚠ Dangerous level

    At this level blackouts, vomiting and falls become likely, and alcohol poisoning is possible.

    • Stop drinking alcohol and switch to water.
    • Don't stay alone, and don't drive.
    • If someone can't be woken, breathes slowly or vomits while drowsy: call 112 and lay them on their side.
